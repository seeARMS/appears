H2O-Lightning-4B (text only, 4-bit)

H2O.ai created the H2O-Lightning-4B decision model, fine-tuned from Qwen3.5-4B by the
Qwen team (Alibaba Cloud).
Source: https://huggingface.co/h2oai/h2o-lightning-4b
Revision: acaf0d4ea251e54de928c75ef4352670d33192d3 (v1.2.3)
License: Apache License 2.0; see LICENSE.txt in this distribution.

Appears converts that checkpoint with mlx-vlm to MLX's 4-bit affine format (group size
64) and keeps only the language model's tensors; the vision encoder is omitted. The
conversion is quantization only, without training. Tokenizer and configuration files
come from the same conversion. appears-model.json records the source, revision, tools
and file checksums; the app pins and verifies the complete download.
