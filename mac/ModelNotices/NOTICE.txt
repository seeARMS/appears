EmbeddingGemma 2 (text and vision)

Google DeepMind created the EmbeddingGemma 2 model.
Source: https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2
Revision: 914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b
License: Apache License 2.0; see LICENSE.txt in this distribution.

Appears packages the text and vision tensors from that checkpoint into separate
safetensors files and omits the audio encoder. Retained tensor values are copied
byte for byte, without quantization or training. Tokenizer and configuration
files are copied from the same revision. appears-model.json records the source,
revision and file checksums; the app pins and verifies the complete download.
